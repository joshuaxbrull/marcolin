# Photo direction · 8 September 2026

The seasoned-rider concept is the first creative recommendation: one large face, glasses over the eyes, and recognizable Harley motorcycle details. In a busy event setting, this gives the card a simple visual focus. This is a design judgment, not a measured conversion result or a claim about the exact Ocean City audience.

Harley-Davidson's [2026 H.O.G. rally feature](https://www.harley-davidson.com/us/en/content/stories/2026-hog-rally-highlights.html) emphasizes riding, friendship and shared experiences. Its images include women, pairs and groups of riders. The [brand's current experiences page](https://www.harley-davidson.com/us/en/content/stories/experiences.html) also covers coastal rallies, custom motorcycles and women's riding events. These support testing a seasoned rider, a woman rider and a couple rather than relying on one narrow portrayal of attendees.

The generated scenes use parked bikes, coastal daylight, familiar riding clothes and clear eyewear. Faces turn toward the contact panel. The large Harley-Davidson name remains live text across the photo bar so that it prints sharply. The left third uses the official vector shield, and Mike Brull is smaller to give the brand priority. Backgrounds are creative coastal settings, not verified photographs of Ocean City.

Generation mode: built-in image_gen, one initial generation and one reference-guided edit per concept. [photo-concepts.json](photo-concepts.json) records the exact initial prompts, refinement prompts, references and saved asset paths. Initial artwork is preserved alongside each refined output. Supplied images were copied without retouching.

The product screenshots guide appearance. Generated depictions should still be compared with the actual frame before press approval; they are not catalogue photographs or evidence of ANSI performance. [Marcolin's Harley-Davidson licensing announcement](https://www.marcolin.com/de/news/en-harley-davidson-e-marcolin-consolidano-la-loro-partnership-con-il-rinnovo-dellaccordo-di-licenza/) describes optical frames, sunglasses and a protective Performance category. The card keeps its existing qualification that ANSI impact standards apply to select Performance Series models and adds the user's requested RX availability note.

The back now places Ocean City to the right of the coastal pins, with a short leader to the unchanged geographic marker. The extra map width preserves the previous scale and pin sizes. Both back colors share that layout and the same QR target.
